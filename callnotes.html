2. Introduce Finnovate say it naturally

        
Do not read this out word for word. Cover these five beats in your own words.

        
    
          
  1. What we are.
    "Finnovate is a financial plan led investment advisory firm."
    2. 
          
  2. We start with your goals.
    "What that means is, we start by understanding your goals. It could be retirement planning, your child's education, or buying a house, and in how many years you want each one."
    3. 
          
  3. The plan gives you a number.
    "We feed that into our financial planning platform, and what it gives us is how much you should be saving to reach all of those goals. So we get a clear number. Say it works out to ₹1 lakh a month."
    4. 
          
  4. Then we tell you where to invest it.
    "Now we already know your goals and your time horizon, so where you invest that ₹1 lakh comes to our investment advisory service. That is where we advise which mutual funds to buy, which stocks, and what mix."
    5. 
          
  5. Close the intro.
    "That's Finnovate for you. The plan first, and then the investments that deliver the plan."
    6. 
        

        

          
Then ask for the meeting: "Is this something you are looking for? If yes, I'll assign a certified financial planner to walk you through it in detail, based on your current portfolio size."

          
Then, straight away: "May I know what your current investment size is?" Do not pause here. The planner is assigned on the basis of this number, so it follows naturally from what you just said.

          
Once you have covered all five beats, tick Intro on the form.

        

      


      

        
3. If they ask about the fee

        
Say: "Our advisory fees start from ₹40,000."

        
    
          
  • Quote it only if the lead asks. Do not volunteer the fee.
    • 
          
  • Give the starting number, never a final number. The planner confirms the exact fee after seeing the portfolio and the scope of work.
    • 
          
  • Do not discount on the call.
    • 
          
  • If they push back: "That covers a full financial plan plus advice through the year, not a one time report. The planner will show you exactly what is included."
    •

Handling common objections

        
    
          
  • "Just send me the details on WhatsApp."
    "I'll send it right away. The details make far more sense with the planner walking you through your own numbers, so let me pencil in a slot as well."
    • 
          
  • "I already have an advisor / my CA handles this."
    "That's good. We work differently: we start with a written financial plan and we're paid a fee by you, not a commission by a fund. Most people find it useful as a second opinion."
    • 
          
  • "Are you selling insurance or a product?"
    "No. We're a SEBI registered advisory firm. We charge a fee and advise, we don't earn on what you buy."
    • 
          
  • "I'll think about it."
    "Of course. Shall I hold a slot for later this week? It's free to cancel."
    • 
          
  • "How much return will I get?"
    "I can't promise a return, and nobody honestly can. What the plan tells you is how much you need to save to reach your goals."
    • 
        

      


      

        
Never say compliance

        

          
No guaranteed or assured returns. Not even as a rough promise.

          
No specific fund or stock recommendation on this call. That is the planner's job, after the plan.

          
Never quote a fee below ₹40,000 and never negotiate it on the call.

          
Do not pressure. If the lead says no, close politely and log it.
